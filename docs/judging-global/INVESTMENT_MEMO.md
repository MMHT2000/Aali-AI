# Investment Memorandum: Aali AI

Fund / Panel: Global Impact & Frontier Resilience Capital
Date: 2026-10-10
Recommendation: **Fund After Conditions ($750k Seed)**

---

## 1. Investment Thesis
Aali AI has built the most technically disciplined disaster decision-support platform seen in the climate resilience sector. In flash floods where 90%+ of terrestrial mobile cell towers collapse, traditional web and consumer apps fail completely. Aali's proprietary 42-byte ASCII disaster packet and deterministic decision rules bridge the critical gap between stranded citizens, satellite-to-phone links, and rescue boat dispatchers.

At $0.0002 per processed SOS distress report and a proven 311 req/s throughput on single-core compute, the technology offers unmatched gross margins and life-saving leverage.

## 3. Core Business & Monetization Model

### Revenue Stream 1: B2G / NGO Institutional Command License
- **Target Customers**: Ministry of Disaster Management (MoDMR), IFRC / BDRCS, UNDP.
- **Pricing**: $25,000 annual recurring software license per district control room, including GIS map server hosting, multi-tenant RBAC, and dedicated 24/7 disaster season support.

### Revenue Stream 2: B2B Telecom Emergency Gateway Integration
- **Target Customers**: Mobile Network Operators (Grameenphone, Banglalink, Robi) and Satellite NTN providers (Starlink Direct-to-Cell).
- **Pricing**: $0.005 per routed and deduplicated emergency packet, enabling carriers to fulfill national emergency regulatory compliance mandates at a fraction of internal development cost.

## 4. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow B2G Government Sales Cycle** | High | Close bridge contracts with international NGOs (BDRCS, World Vision, CARE) that operate on fast discretionary disaster grant budgets. |
| **Citizen App Installation Friction** | High | Pivot citizen reporting to zero-install SMS 1090 gateway and zero-rated carrier web portals; preserve mobile app for registered field volunteers. |
| **Big Tech Platform Encroachment** | Medium | Defend through localized dialect NLP models, deep integrations with Upazila Disaster Management Committees (UDMC), and local volunteer networks. |

## 5. Conditions Precedent to Disbursing Funds (Milestones)

The investment syndicate will release the $750,000 seed tranche upon fulfillment of the following three conditions within 90 days:
1. **Binding NGO Pilot Agreement**: Secure a signed Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) or Letter of Intent (LOI) from Bangladesh Red Crescent Society (BDRCS) or Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP) for pre-monsoon field deployment in Feni.
2. **Carrier SMS Ingestion Sandbox**: Finalize an official carrier sandbox testing agreement with at least one Bangladesh mobile operator (e.g., Banglalink) for short-code 1090 packet routing.
3. **Multi-District Tenancy Architecture**: Ship multi-tenant district partitioning across the NestJS API and MariaDB database schema, enabling independent control room deployments for Feni and Sylhet.
