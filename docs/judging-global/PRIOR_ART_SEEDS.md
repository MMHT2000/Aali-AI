# Prior-art research seeds

The Prior-art analyst researches these **before** judging and writes `02_prior_art.md`. These are starting points, not conclusions. Do not assume what any product can do. For each item record: what it is, who it serves, what it does for flood or disaster warning, SOS or coordination, where it is available, its limits, a URL and the retrieval date. Facts you cannot confirm are marked `UNVERIFIED`. Add every relevant system you find.

## Warning and forecasting
- Google Flood Hub and Google flood forecasting; Google Android earthquake alerts
- National flood warning systems: Bangladesh FFWC and BMD, India CWC, other national agencies
- Cell-broadcast alert systems used in other countries (for example the US, the EU, Japan) and the standard families behind them
- Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) and how agencies use it

## SOS and emergency messaging
- Phone-maker satellite emergency features (Apple, Google, others) and carrier direct-to-cell offerings, including emergency uses and recent disaster deployments
- Emergency numbers and apps in Bangladesh (national emergency line, Fire Service, Red Crescent, DDM apps)
- Offline and mesh messaging tools used in disasters

## Coordination and crisis mapping
- Ushahidi and similar crowd-reporting platforms
- Humanitarian OpenStreetMap, IFRC digital tools, OCHA data platforms
- Incident-management and dispatch products used by emergency services

## Community early warning
- Bangladesh Cyclone Preparedness Programme and similar volunteer networks
- Community-based flood warning projects in South Asia

## Required output
1. A comparison table: Aali versus each relevant alternative on warning, SOS, offline operation, coordination, local language, cost and availability in Bangladesh.
2. A short, honest **"what is genuinely new"** section: which Aali capabilities no surveyed system provides, stated narrowly.
3. A **"what is already solved better elsewhere"** section.
4. Any risk that a larger platform could ship the same thing quickly.
