## 5. Panel Chair's Closing Verdict
Aali AI is a remarkable engineering triumph that demonstrates the highest level of architectural integrity, ethical restraint, and domain safety seen at this global hackathon. By explicitly decoupling non-deterministic AI from life-critical triage and warning logic, the team has built a resilient, honest platform that works under real-world catastrophic tower blackouts.

To advance from a **69/100 Finalist Contender** to a **90+ Grand Prize Winner and Fundable Company**, the team must now solve the friction of citizen distribution (moving beyond mobile app pre-installs to zero-rated carrier USSD/SMS flows) and establish a viable public-sector procurement model with relief agencies and telecom operators.
