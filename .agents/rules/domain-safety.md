# Aali domain safety
- Aali is decision support, not an operational emergency service.
- Keep warning level, confidence, silent-village detection and boat priority in deterministic, testable domain functions. AI must never decide those values.
- Require authorized human approval for warning publication and human review for duplicate merge/split and dispatch recommendations.
- Never infer safety from no reports and never block/delete an SOS solely because confidence is low.
- Label reconstructed flood data, demo seeds, gateway forwarding, satellite SMS, carrier integrations, cell broadcast and external sends as simulated unless independently proven.
- A browser's offline queue/copy/SMS-composer handoff is not proof that an SMS or satellite message was transmitted. Only server/provider acknowledgement can change a state to accepted/delivered, with the provider type visible.
- AI may extract fields, suggest text similarity and draft messages. All output must be schema-validated and editable; provide deterministic/manual fallback.
- Keep phone numbers, auth material, exact locations and raw personal text out of logs and remote AI requests. Use synthetic data for demo.
